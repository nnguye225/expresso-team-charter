Client Overview

Meet our client.

Mark Garcia · Owner of Sidoh Cafe

Mark is exploring opportunities for future growth. He uses research about local communities, demographics, nearby coffee shops, and customer preferences to understand where a new location could succeed.

Community and marketing

Sidoh Cafe has collaborated with local influencers and running club leaders, and Mark is open to more community partnerships. He is interested in promoting the business through both digital channels and traditional methods, and in highlighting local artists, vendors, and entrepreneurs.

Goal & Scope

Project Goal and Scope.

Goal

Use demographic and community data to identify potential areas where a new coffee shop could be successful, while building community connections, developing partnerships, and using digital and traditional marketing to promote the business.

Out of Scope

  • Choosing the final location for Sidoh Cafe’s expansion.
  • Managing the business’s ongoing marketing or advertising after the project ends.
  • Running day-to-day coffee shop operations, such as inventory, stocking shelves, or handling customer transactions.

Sidoh Team Personas

Meet the people behind the cafe.

Three personas representing members of the Sidoh Cafe team.

Sandy Store Supervisor

Sandy oversees the store and its day-to-day operations.

Role / Context

She tracks inventory deliveries and checks that service and business operations keep running smoothly.

Goals

  • Account for inventory truck deliveries.
  • Maintain service for customers and the business.

Frustrations

  • Deliveries arriving late or not arriving.
  • Equipment malfunctioning at a bad time.
  • Business slowing down.

What Success Looks Like

Every station runs smoothly and customers are happy with the service they receive. Sandy checks on the service and business to help keep operations stable.

Oscar Cashier

Oscar handles customer orders, payments, and the front counter.

Role / Context

He serves customers at the counter and communicates orders to coworkers.

Goals

  • Take customer orders.
  • Provide customer service and handle payments.
  • Communicate orders to coworkers.

Frustrations

  • A customer may be having a bad day and not be in the mood to interact.
  • The payment system may stop working, leaving cash as the available payment option.

What Success Looks Like

At the end of his shift, Oscar checks the register, restocks supplies, and cleans his station. He briefs the incoming cashier before finishing his shift.

Becky Barista / Customer Service

Becky prepares and serves orders while helping customers.

Role / Context

She prepares orders, serves them to customers, and keeps her station stocked.

Goals

  • Prepare and serve customer orders.
  • Restock supplies for her station.
  • Provide customer service.

Frustrations

  • Orders changing at the last minute.
  • Orders piling up.

What Success Looks Like

Orders are served on time, and customers enjoy what they receive. At the end of her shift, Becky cleans her station, briefs the next barista, and heads home.
